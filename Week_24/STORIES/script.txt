SQL tables:
users (user_id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY UNIQUE, 
    username TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE, 
    email TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE, 
    password TEXT ,
    avatar IMAGE, 
    isLoggedIn BOOLEAN,
    isPrivate BOOLEAN,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE),
    friends TEXT (list of user_id`s of friends within the app),
    coauthors TEXT (list of people who you collaborate your stories with)
    isActive BOOLEAN (true as default)

stories (story_id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY UNIQUE, 
    version INTEGER (looks like story_id+1,2,3 etc.),
    header TEXT,
    story TEXT NOT NULL, 
    author_id INTEGER (linked with users->id),
    collaborators TEXT (list of collaborators` user_id`s)
    ); - each edition creates new story version - story_id remains the same (e.g. story_id 1212), while
        a version changes (e.g. 1212_1 is the primanry version, 1212_2 is the next one)
